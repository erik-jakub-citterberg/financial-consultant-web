## How OVB / broker advice is paid for
The client consultation is free; the adviser is paid a commission (provízia) by the product
provider (bank, insurer, investment company), not by the client. Adviser sites advertise
"bezplatná konzultácia" but almost never disclose the commission mechanism — so an honest one-line
note that Erika is paid by the institution, not the client, is a small trust differentiator.

## How Slovak sites present pricing
Accounting/tax SZČO sites almost universally publish a concrete cenník (fixed list or "od X €")
plus a per-doklad rate and monthly minimum, with a "finálna cena podľa náročnosti" caveat.
"Cena na mieru" with no numbers is rare and reads as evasive. Financial-advisory sites do the
opposite: no numbers, just a free first consultation.

## Recommendation: hybrid model
- Tax/accounting side: public cenník with "od" prices + per-doklad rate + monthly minimum,
  one-line "finálna cena podľa náročnosti a počtu dokladov" caveat. Transparency builds trust with
  a small local clientele and matches every competitor; silence reads as expensive or hiding.
- OVB advisory side: no price list, "bezplatná, nezáväzná konzultácia" CTA, plus the honest line
  that she is paid by the financial institution, not the client.
- Avoid a fully number-free site: for a YMYL, trust-driven local audience it lowers conversion.
